## Definitions (per authorization lineage, per agency-local month)

`Authorized = Billable + Rounding + Pending + Reserved + Hours left`

| Name | What it is | Source rows |
|---|---|---|
| Billable | Confirmed billable minutes: signed visits plus minutes accepted from a late confirmation | `service_visits` (status signed), `visit_review_decisions` (accept_late_confirmation) |
| Rounding | Approved billing rounding adjustments, positive or negative, net of voids. Own line, counted once. Shown only when not zero | `billing_rounding_adjustments` |
| Pending | Worked but not yet billable. (a) billable minutes carried by a visit in administrator review; (b) shift time held while the linked visit awaits confirmation or is in review with nothing billable; (c) worked minutes of an ended visit awaiting confirmation or in review that has no billable minutes and holds no reservation | `service_visits`, `authorization_reservations` |
| Reserved | Scheduled time not yet delivered: shifts ahead, under way, and shifts that ended with no visit record (awaiting a person's decision, including "awaiting record") | `authorization_reservations` via `reservation_bucket` |
| Hours left | Authorized minus the four above. Signed. Negative is displayed as **Over limit** with the amount over | computed |
| Worked, not billable | Reconciliation line OUTSIDE the equation: worked minutes of signed and in-review visits beyond what is counted as Billable or Pending | `service_visits` |

Hours left and Over limit are per authorization. A total across authorizations (Home, the reporting section, the client calendar) adds Hours left over the authorizations that still have time and Over limit over those that are over, side by side. They are never netted: Authorized - Billable - Rounding - Pending - Reserved = Hours left - Over limit on a total, and the screens say so.
Old columns (`billable_minutes`, `remaining_minutes`, `committed_minutes`, `over_limit_minutes`, `reserved_*`) keep their exact meaning; the new columns are `hours_*` (additive). The web now reads `hours_*` and falls back to the old fields only for an older server.

## What was wrong (proved by an independent recompute, `hours-accounting.db.test.ts`)
The old identity `authorized = billable + adjustment + reserved + remaining` always held, because `remaining` was defined as the residual. It could not show a gap, so the gaps were hidden:

1. **Gap: an unscheduled visit awaiting confirmation or in review (no shift, no reservation) was in no bucket.** Example (test): authorized 600, visit worked 45 awaiting confirmation. Old: Billable 0, Reserved 0, Remaining 600, so 45 worked minutes appeared nowhere. New: Pending 45, Hours left 555.
2. **Billable included visits that are only in review.** A 60-minute visit in review on a 130-minute shift was shown as Billable 60. The owner definition puts it in Pending (now Billable 0, Pending 60, Hours left 540; the old `billable_minutes` field still says 60).
3. **Worked time beyond billable was invisible.** Worked 150 on a 100-minute authorization: capped billable 100, 50 minutes appeared nowhere. New: "Worked, not billable 50" (a visible line, not part of the equation, because those minutes are not part of Authorized). With 60 more reserved ahead, that authorization reads Over limit 60 (Hours left -60), while another authorization with 600 unused stays at 600 (test "overages are per authorization").
4. **"Reserved" mixed two things.** Shift time held for a visit awaiting confirmation or review is Pending (worked, not yet billable); Reserved is only not-yet-delivered time. The old `reserved_minutes` total is unchanged; the split is `hours_pending_held_minutes` and `hours_reserved_minutes`.
5. Defensive: `reservation_bucket` now returns no bucket for a reservation whose linked visit is already signed (it is Billable; 0013 releases it at signing, so the test cannot reach a case; the guard prevents a future double count). The 0071 version of the function (awaiting_record for delivered_unverified) was kept and extended, not replaced.

**No double count was found in current flows.** Checked by test: review visit with billable minutes plus its reservation (0052 holds), accepted late confirmation plus its reservation (0049 holds), correction (the original is `corrected`, only the new visit counts), rounding positive and negative (counted once), ended shift reconciled as no service (leaves Reserved).

**The review document example** (Authorized 20,162.3 h, Billable 63.3 h, Hours left 20,067.9 h, 31.1 h unexplained, only Over limit shown; worked 66.5 h vs billable 63.3 h) could not be reproduced here (no production data). Its pattern matches the causes above: 31.1 h is Reserved/Pending/Rounding time that was not displayed, and the 3.2 h between worked and billable is the cap/uncounted time. After 0079 each of those has its own line; run the test-environment numbers through the Details view to confirm.

## Intentional caregiver vs office differences (2.3)

### 1. Pending hours and the scheduling guards: the gap was real and is closed
Finding (failed on 0080): authorized 600, an unscheduled visit awaiting confirmation or in review worked 45 (Pending 45, Hours left 555). `create_shift` accepted 560 minutes (45 + 560 = 605, Over limit 5), `reschedule_shift` accepted 560, `create_shift_series` accepted a 560-minute occurrence, and `assert_shift_authorization_covered` (the check behind office assignment 0065 and pickup 0063) accepted a 300-minute open shift after 400 Pending minutes (700 of 600). The guards read `authorization_month_usage + authorization_month_reserved`; the unreserved Pending minutes were in neither, though 0079 had put them on the screen. The care-request check (`care_request_reserved_usage`) had the same blind spot.
Fix: `ogevia.visit_unreserved_pending_minutes(visit)` is the ONE definition (an ended visit awaiting confirmation, or in review with nothing billable, no accepted late confirmation, no review closed as not billable, and no live reservation in its month). The Hours breakdown (screen) and `authorization_month_pending_unreserved` (guard) both use it, and `authorization_month_reserved` and `care_request_reserved_usage` add it. Every guard that reads the reserved total (create, reschedule, series, make-up, assign, pickup, care request) now sees it. A test asserts that for every hours scenario `authorized - (usage + reserved total) = displayed Hours left`, so the screen and the guard cannot drift apart.
Not double counted: a pending visit that holds a reservation is counted once (the reservation); a visit carrying billable minutes is in usage; an accepted late confirmation is in usage; a closed review is not counted here (below).
Concurrency preserved: no lock, reservation write, lock order or idempotency path was touched; every guard already takes the authorization-lineage lock before it reads these figures. Tests: two simultaneous 300-minute reservations race for the 555 left: exactly one succeeds, the other is refused with 409; six simultaneous 100-minute reservations: exactly five succeed (500), Hours left 55, never Over limit; the same idempotency key sent twice at once creates one shift. Residual risk (documented, not changed): `end_service_visit` does not take the authorization lineage lock, so a visit that ends at the very instant a shift is being scheduled can be missed by that one check (the confirmation-time cap still applies); closing that needs a lock in `end_service_visit` and is a separate change.
Visible effect on a screen: scheduling and assignment messages that report "available" time now include Pending time (they use the same figure as Hours left). The Pending term appears in the "remaining" returned by `create_shift` and `reschedule_shift`.

### 2. Reviews closed as not billable: a distinct state, no policy change
Why the hold stays: `decide_visit_review` writes only a decision row (0031: "the visit row, its times and its billable minutes are never changed by it"). The visit stays in `administrator_review`, so `ogevia.reservation_bucket` keeps answering `held_review` for its shift's reservation. A reservation is released only at signing (0013), by `cancel_shift`/a series change (both refused while a visit is linked: "This shift is no longer scheduled" / "A visit is linked to this shift"), or by `reconcile_ended_shift` (refuses a shift that has a visit: "A visit exists"). Nothing releases it today.
Labelling (policy-neutral): `ogevia.hours_breakdown` now separates it. New function `reservation_closed_not_billable`. Pending no longer includes it. New breakdown/summary fields: `hours_closed_not_billable_held_minutes` (what a closed review still holds: its shift reservation, or billable minutes it still carries) and `hours_closed_not_billable_worked_minutes` (its worked minutes, visible, outside the equation). The held part is counted in Reserved (`hours_reserved_minutes`, `hours_left_minutes` and the caregiver office figure include it), so Authorized = Billable + Rounding + Pending + Reserved + Hours left stays exact, and the scheduling guards and the old `reserved_*` columns are unchanged. Example (test): shift 130, worked 60, closed: Pending 130 -> 0, Reserved 0 -> 130 (of which closed, not billable 130), Worked, not billable 60, Hours left 470 both before and after. An unscheduled visit closed as not billable: Pending 45 -> 0, Worked, not billable 45, closed worked 45, Hours left 555 -> 600.
Web follow-up (not done here): the summary/Details view should show a line "Closed, not billable (held)" from `hours_closed_not_billable_held_minutes` inside Reserved and "Worked, not billable" already shows the worked minutes; Home, the chain report and the continuity pack carry the held time inside Reserved without a separate line.
**POLICY QUESTION (owner, billing policy; not decided here): should closing a review as not billable release the shift reservation?**
- Release (recommended): the office has decided the visit is not billable, so its 130 scheduled minutes are neither delivered nor billable; keeping them locks 130 of authorized time that can never be billed. Consequence: Hours left rises by the hold (470 -> 600 in the example), the time can be rescheduled to someone else, and the audit trail keeps the closed review, the decision and the worked minutes (nothing is deleted). Needs a new audited release reason on the reservation (for example `review_closed_not_billable`), done inside `decide_visit_review` under the same shift/reservation/lineage lock order as `reconcile_ended_shift`, and a reversible path when a decision is revoked.
- Keep (today): the authorization stays reduced until the shift is cancelled, which is impossible while the visit exists, so the hold is permanent. Consequence: the agency cannot re-use that time even though nothing billable was delivered; the line "Closed, not billable" at least makes the amount visible.
- An unscheduled visit closed as not billable already consumes nothing (no reservation). That is the same policy as "release".

### 3. CPR/TB: a pending document is not a qualifying document
`readiness_checks` (the one gate for create, reschedule, series, make-up, assign, pickup, care request and visit start, through `assert_ready_for_work`) had a branch (0055, kept in 0060 and 0078) that turned a missing or uploaded-but-unverified (`awaiting_review`) CPR/TB into status `pending` (not blocking) for 14 days after the hire date. 0081 removes that branch: missing, awaiting review, rejected, expired, expiring before the work ends, expiry date missing and set-up missing all give status `blocked` (Blocked when the agency's enforcement is active, Warning when report-only). Only a VERIFIED, CURRENT document gives `ok`. The messages are unchanged ("CPR missing.", "CPR needs review.", "CPR expired."). Enforcement is not activated by this migration; the tests switch it on inside a synthetic database only.
Unchanged by design: the compliance HOLD (`compliance_blockers`, `compliance_grace`, the sweep, the stricter reviewer permissions) still waits 14 days before it opens an automatic hold; inside the period a new hire with a missing document has no hold, but cannot be assigned or start a visit once enforcement is active. Report-only mode still allows the work and shows the Warning.
Policy decision for the owner: this makes the 14 days irrelevant to scheduling for an enforcing agency (a new hire cannot be scheduled or start a visit until CPR and TB are verified). If a short onboarding exception for training or orientation shifts is wanted, it should be an explicit, audited, one-shot exception like the availability override, not a silent grace.

### 4. Worked, not billable: it cannot permit overbooking
Decided by test. Worked time exceeds billable time only when confirmation caps billable at `authorized - usage` (`confirm_service_visit` 0057), that is, when the authorization is already used up by that visit: worked 150 on an authorization of 100 gives billable 100 carried in review (Pending 100) and Worked, not billable 50; Hours left is 0 before and after the office accepts the capped minutes, and not even a 15-minute shift can be scheduled (refused). So the uncounted excess cannot create room. Intentionally outside consumption: (a) the excess beyond the cap (billing cap semantic: billable never exceeds the authorization); (b) worked time of an unscheduled visit closed as not billable (no reservation; consumes nothing, so the full authorization can be scheduled again). A signed visit releases its whole shift reservation (0013) and only its billable minutes stay consumed, because scheduled time is not delivered time; a visit that differs from its shift by more than the agency's variance rule goes to review instead (0050), where its billable minutes are Pending (not tested separately here: the test helper resizes the shift to the worked time). Decisions for the owner: (i) confirm that a visit closed as not billable should free its authorization time (recommended: yes, it is not billable); (ii) confirm that the excess beyond the cap must never be billable even if the authorization is later increased (today it stays Worked, not billable; a later amendment adds room for new visits only).
Rounding stays one explicit line, counted once: in the hours breakdown (`rounding_minutes`), in `authorization_month_usage` (60 = 50 + 10) and not in the reserved or pending terms; test: Billable 50 + Rounding 10 + Pending 45 + Reserved 100 + Hours left 395 = 600, and the guard refuses 545 minutes against 540 left and accepts 540.
