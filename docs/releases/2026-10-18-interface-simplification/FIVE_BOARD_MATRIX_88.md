Discrepancies and judgment calls:
- **Board 5 yields 9 + 5 items, not 14 distinct decisions.** The approved page has nine numbered "Approved scope and rules" plus one rule (rule 1) that names five sections. The earlier comparison only cites B5-R2, R3, R4, R6, R7, R8, which fits R1..R9 = the nine rules in page order. To reach 14, I used R1 = the overall five-section structure (rule 1), R2..R9 = rules 2..9, and R10..R14 = the five sections one by one (Billing Readiness, Authorized vs Delivered Hours, Agency Performance, Reports and Exports, Compliance and Audit Evidence). R10..R14 overlap rule 1 and several other rules by design. This mapping is my reconstruction; the earlier document never lists R1, R5, R9..R14.
- Board 1 and Board 4 page titles still say "Review Pending" and "Decision Register - Pending", but each page ends with an owner final approval, which supersedes them (Board 1: 12 requirements + consolidated groups; Board 4: 13 decisions, final approval is Decision 14, not counted). Board 5 title says "Proposed"; its final entry is "Design approved, Option A".
- Board 3: 18 requirements and 6 exceptions as stated. The six exceptions are numbered E1..E6 in page order: strict work-hour limits, recurring conflicts, reassignment, late cancellations and partial visits, carryover, urgent coverage.
- Board 2: 19 decisions plus the accounting rule = 20, as stated. The page's "screenshot audit" and "Claude Code instruction" blocks are not requirements and are not counted.
- The "Status then" column: the earlier comparison gives areas, not a per-ID table. "CONFLICT (section 4 #n)" means the row is the named subject of conflict n; "named in conflict #n" means the row is one of several source rows of a conflict and its own status is not stated; omissions come from section 6 of the earlier document. Where nothing is said, the cell reads "not stated". The earlier counts (13 / 46 / 12 / 13 / 4) cannot be reproduced row by row.
- "Browser" below means the Chromium runs on the local harness (synthetic data) described in README sections 12-15. Home, the attention pages, client detail and Schedule were run on earlier commits (appearance matrix 3456/3456, repeated on this commit). For requirements 6-10 a scenario run on the final local build exercised: the caregiver's request for an open shift (including a request refused by the 8 h / 40 h policy with its plain reason), the office's request panel and confirmation, the Staffing link to waiting requests, the agency hour-limit banner, the repeat form saving the dates that fit and listing every rejected date with its reason, and the Billing review page. At 390 px every one of those checks passed (23/23); at 1280 px 21/23 passed, the two misses being order-dependent (the first pass had already used the over-limit shift and created the series; the same behaviour is proven by the 390 px pass). The billing-state words were not exercised in a browser against rows in every state (the sample data holds none): that remains unit-tested only. Not run: a real phone, a screen reader, TEST or production.
